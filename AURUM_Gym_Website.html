Our Story

Born From a Relentless Pursuit

AURUM was founded on a single belief: that serious fitness and serious luxury should not be separate pursuits.

2016 Founded

The Origin

Forged in Hyderabad, Built for Champions

AURUM began in 2016 when Olympic strength coach Rajan Nair returned to Hyderabad after 12 years training athletes in London and Dubai. He saw a gap — world-class coaching trapped inside uninspiring spaces.

He built AURUM to bridge that gap. The name — Latin for gold — captures everything we stand for: the highest standard, enduring value, and the beauty of the refined pursuit of excellence.

01

Excellence

Every detail, every session, every interaction — held to the gold standard.

02

Integrity

Science-backed programming. Honest coaching. No shortcuts, ever.

03

Community

A network of high achievers who elevate each other relentlessly.

04

Innovation

Constantly integrating the latest in sports science and recovery technology.

Our Journey

A Decade of Milestones

2016

The Foundation

AURUM opened its first facility in Banjara Hills — 8,000 sq ft, 3 coaches, and 200 founding members.

2018

2020

Digital Innovation

The Flagship

2024
